Modules

Everything you run spreadsheets for, now in one system.

Modular ERP — start with what you need, scale to full enterprise. Each module shares the same data layer, permissions, and audit logs. No more duplicate entry.

Module 01 • Finance●
💰

Finance & Accounting

Real-time ledger, automated invoicing, expense tracking and financial reporting that closes your month-end in hours, not days.

  • Chart of accounts, journal, P&L, balance sheet
  • Automated invoicing & payment reconciliation
  • Multi-currency & tax compliance
  • Role-based approval for expenses
Module 02 • Inventory●
📦

Inventory & Warehouse

Track stock across locations, set reorder levels, manage batches and expiry — with barcode scanning and stock audit built-in.

  • Multi-warehouse stock tracking
  • Low stock alerts & auto-reorder
  • Batch, serial, expiry management
  • Barcode + stock count app
Module 03 • HR & Payroll●
👥

HR & Payroll

From onboarding to payroll — manage employees, leave, attendance, performance and payslips in one compliant system.

  • Employee database & document vault
  • Leave, attendance, shifts
  • Payroll with taxes & deductions
  • Payslip portal for staff
Module 04 • Sales & CRM●
📈

Sales & CRM

Pipeline, quotes, orders and customer history in one place. Sales sees inventory, finance sees payments, support sees tickets.

  • Leads, deals, pipeline stages
  • Quotes, orders, delivery tracking
  • Customer 360° history
  • Automated follow-ups & reminders
Module 05 • Procurement●
🤝

Procurement & Vendors

Vendor management, purchase requests, approvals, POs and goods receipt — with 3-way matching to stop overpayment.

  • Vendor database & performance score
  • PR → Approval → PO → GRN flow
  • 3-way matching (PO, GRN, Invoice)
  • Spend analysis & budgeting
Module 06 • Operations●
⚙️

Manufacturing & Ops

Bill of materials, work orders, production tracking and quality checks — built for small factories and assembly teams.

  • BOM & work order management
  • Production planning & tracking
  • Quality checks & rejection flow
  • Costing per unit & variance

Delivery Process

From process mapping to go-live in 4 phases.

We don't code first. We map your current process, then design the ERP around it.

Phase 01 • Discovery●

Process mapping

We shadow your team for 3-5 days, map every spreadsheet, approval and handoff.

  • Stakeholder interviews & current SOP audit
  • Spreadsheet & tool inventory
  • Pain points & bottlenecks map
  • ERP readiness score
Phase 02 • Blueprint●

Blueprint & prototype

Figma prototype of your ERP with real data structure. You click through before we code.

  • Data model & permission matrix
  • Clickable Figma prototype
  • Module priority & phasing
  • Integration plan (existing tools)
Phase 03 • Build & Migrate●

Build + data migration

We build in sprints, migrate your historical data, and run parallel with old system.

  • Agile sprints with demo every week
  • Historical data cleaning & migration
  • API integrations (bank, accounting, etc.)
  • Parallel run with spreadsheets
Phase 04 • Train & Go-live●

Training & hypercare

Go-live with on-site training, StudyPod videos, and 30-day support where we fix anything same-day.

  • Role-based training (sales, warehouse, finance)
  • Video library via StudyPod
  • 30-day hypercare & SLA
  • Continuous improvement backlog
